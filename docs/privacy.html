Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

OneClick Optimizer is made by Ashutosh Roy ("we"). Contact: mitovoid.contact@gmail.com.

The short version

The app works on your PC and keeps what it finds on your PC. It has no analytics, no tracking and no ads, and we never see your files, your scan results or the programs you run. This website counts visits without cookies.

What the app keeps on your PC

Scan history, the undo history, your settings and (if you bought Pro) your encrypted license key are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OneClickOptimizer. They never leave your PC. Uninstalling with "also remove my data" deletes them.
